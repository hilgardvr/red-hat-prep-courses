# Privacy notice

This page explains what the RHCSA Field Guide collects, why, where it is kept, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be readable rather than legalistic, but it covers the rights you have under the **Protection of Personal Information Act** (POPIA, South Africa) and, for visitors in Europe, the **General Data Protection Regulation** (GDPR).

**Short version:** the site sets no advertising cookies, the analytics count pages rather than people, and the only personal data collected is the address you type into the subscribe form — which you can have deleted at any time.

## Who is responsible

The site is published by an individual maintainer, not a company. For the purposes of POPIA and the GDPR, that person is the **responsible party** / **data controller** for the small amount of data described here.

Because this is a personal project rather than a business, there is no data protection officer. Requests go to the maintainer directly, using the contact route at the end of this page.

## What is collected, and when

| Situation | What is stored | Why |
|---|---|---|
| You read any page | A page-view count: the URL, the referring site, the browser's language, the screen size, and a coarse country. **No cookie, no IP address, no identifier that follows you between visits.** | To see which modules people actually read, so the next revision improves the right pages |
| You submit the subscribe form | The email address you typed, the first name if you gave one, the time you signed up, and the page you subscribed from | To send the PDF you asked for and occasional course updates |
| You confirm the subscription | The time of the confirmation click | Proof that the request was yours (double opt-in), which is what POPIA and the GDPR expect |
| You open an email | Whether the message was opened, in aggregate | To decide whether email is worth continuing at all |

Nothing else is collected. In particular: no name or address is required to read the site, there is no comment system, no login, and no payment details anywhere on this site.

### Theme preference

The dark/light switch stores your choice in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, is not sent anywhere, and deleting it only means the site forgets which theme you picked.

## Why it is allowed

- **The subscribe list** is processed on the basis of your **consent**, given when you tick the box on the form and confirm the email. You can withdraw that consent at any time by unsubscribing, which deletes the record rather than merely marking it inactive.
- **Page-view counts** are aggregate statistics with no personal identifiers. There is no profile of an individual to consent to.

## Who else sees it

Two service providers process data on the maintainer's behalf. Both are **data processors**: they may only use the data to provide the service, not for their own marketing.

- **The email service provider** that runs the mailing list and delivers the PDF. It stores the address and the signup times, and handles bounces and unsubscribes.
- **The analytics provider** (GoatCounter), which stores aggregate page-view counts. Its data is not linked to the mailing list, and there is no shared identifier between the two.

The site itself is served by **GitHub Pages**, which, like any web host, receives the technical request data needed to serve a page (including an IP address) and handles it under its own privacy statement. The maintainer does not receive or store that data.

No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, and there is no advertising on this site.

## Where it is stored, and for how long

Email addresses are stored by the email provider on servers outside South Africa, most likely in the European Union or the United States. Transfer relies on the provider's standard contractual clauses, which is the usual mechanism for moving data across borders under both POPIA and the GDPR.

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Subscribed email address | Until you unsubscribe, plus up to 30 days in the provider's backups |
| Unsubscribed address | Not kept. Unsubscribing deletes the record |
| Aggregate page views | Indefinitely, because they contain nothing that identifies a person |

If a backup still holds an address after deletion, it is never used again and expires on its own schedule.

## Your rights

You can ask to:

- **see** what is held about you (a copy of the subscription record);
- **correct** an address or name;
- **delete** the record, including from backups where technically possible;
- **withdraw consent**, which ends all email immediately;
- **object** to processing, or ask for the data in a portable format.

One request is enough, and it is free. There is no form to fill in: use the contact route below and say what you want. Requests are answered within 30 days, usually much sooner.

If you believe the data has been handled badly, you may complain to a supervisory authority: the **Information Regulator** in South Africa (<https://inforegulator.org.za>), or, if you are in the European Economic Area, the data protection authority in your country of residence. You do not have to complain to the maintainer first.

## Security

The list is held by a provider that encrypts data in transit and at rest and restricts access to the maintainer's account, which is protected by a strong password and, where the provider supports it, two-factor authentication. The site itself is served over HTTPS. No system is perfect; if a breach ever affects subscribers, they will be told what happened and what to do, as POPIA and the GDPR require.

## Children

The course is written for adults preparing for a professional certification. The subscribe form is not intended for anyone under 16, and no data is knowingly collected from a child.

## Changes to this notice

If what is collected or who processes it changes, this page is updated in the same commit as the change, and the date below moves. Subscribers are told by email when a change is material — for example, a new processor or a new category of data.

## Contact

Privacy requests, corrections, and deletion:

- Open an issue at <https://github.com/hilgardvr/red-hat-prep-courses/issues> and ask for the removal. Do not put your email address in the public issue text — say only that you want your subscription deleted, and you will be contacted privately to confirm.
- For anything that should not be public, use the same issue tracker to ask for a private channel first.

The site itself collects nothing until you use the subscribe form, so if you have never subscribed, there is nothing to delete beyond an anonymous page-view count.
