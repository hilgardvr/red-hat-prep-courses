# The free EX200 starter pack

<p class="subtitle">The official objective checklist and one complete timed mock exam, in a printable PDF you can keep offline.</p>

The course on this site is free and stays free. This pack is the part you take to the exam: a syllabus to audit yourself against, and an exam to sit under time.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-format-list-checks: **Every EX200 objective, mapped**

    ---

    The coverage matrix lists each objective in the official EX200 v10 grouping and links it to the lesson and lab that teach it. Print it, tick what you can do without notes, and you have a study plan.

-   :material-timer-outline: **Mock exam A, with a grader**

    ---

    Two hours, requirements only, no steps — the way the real exam reads. The exam ships with a checker that grades the machine afterwards, including after a reboot.

-   :material-file-pdf-box: **One PDF, A4, no internet needed**

    ---

    About a dozen pages, typeset for paper, no account required to read it and nothing that expires.

## What happens after you subscribe

1. **You get the PDF link immediately** after confirming your address.
2. **You get corrections and new modules** when they land — nothing else.
3. **You can leave whenever you want.** Every message has a one-click unsubscribe link, and the address is deleted rather than kept "just in case".

!!! note "Why ask for an email at all?"
    Because the alternative is a download button nobody measures. Knowing how many people take the pack tells me which module to improve next, and it funds the free course staying free. The lessons themselves never sit behind this form.

## If the email does not arrive

Check the spam folder first — a first message from a new sender often lands there. If it is still missing after a few minutes, the address may have been mistyped; submit the form again. Anything else is a bug worth reporting on the [project issue tracker](https://github.com/hilgardvr/red-hat-prep-courses/issues).
